# Review rubrics

Derived from ARS `quality_rubrics.md` (see NOTICE.md), condensed. All dimensions
0–100. Scores are **ordinal** (85 beats 65), not cardinal (85 does not
guarantee acceptance). Every score needs a one-line behavioral justification.

## Weighted dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | Owner seat |
|---|---|---|
| Originality | 20% | impact |
| Methodological rigor | 25% | methodology |
| Evidence sufficiency | 25% | domain |
| Argument coherence | 15% | all |
| Writing quality | 15% | all |

## Decision mapping (weighted average)

| Score | Decision |
|---|---|
| ≥ 80 | Accept |
| 65–79 | Minor revision |
| 50–64 | Major revision |
| < 50 | Reject |

**Pass bar for revision loops**: a revision loop runs only if the aggregate is
below 80 or any P0 concern is open. Early-stop when a round moves the aggregate
< 3 points with no open P0.

## Band anchors (abbreviated)

- **Originality** — 90+: opens a new direction, no prior work on the exact
  question; 75+: novel method or novel application; 60+: incremental extension
  with new data/context; 45+: near-replication; <45: no discernible contribution.
- **Methodological rigor** — 90+: design perfectly aligned to the RQ, validity
  threats addressed, powered stats, reproducible; 75+: minor gaps; 60+: validity
  concerns, missing effect sizes/CIs; 45+: significant flaws; <45: invalidating
  flaws.
- **Evidence sufficiency** — 90+: >40 sources, 80%+ peer-reviewed,
  triangulated, counter-evidence engaged; 75+: 25–40 sources, 70%+
  peer-reviewed; 60+: 15–25 sources; 45+: <15 sources or several unsupported
  claims; <45: severely under-sourced. (Counts assume a full paper; scale for
  sections.)
- **Argument coherence** — 90+: problem→gap→RQ→method→findings→implications
  with no jumps, counterarguments pre-empted; 60+: visible but disconnected;
  <45: conclusions don't follow.
- **Writing quality** — 90+: precise, professional, zero errors; 60+:
  serviceable but verbose/imprecise in places; <45: unacceptable.

## Concern tags

`P0` = blocking (invalid inference, fabricated/retracted citation, claim the
evidence cannot support). `P1` = major (must fix for acceptance). `P2` = minor.
